This is the first post on **Dian's Notes**.

I will use this space for technical experiments, reading notes, useful tools, and ideas worth keeping. Instead of leaving fragments scattered across different apps, I want to turn them into clear, searchable articles that can improve over time.

## Why this blog exists

Writing is not only a way to publish; it is also a way to think. A long-lived personal knowledge blog helps me:

- turn solved problems into reusable references;
- test whether I truly understand an idea;
- preserve the evolution of knowledge with version control;
- own a personal space independent of any content platform.

More topics and articles will arrive over time. The design and structure can evolve with the content.
